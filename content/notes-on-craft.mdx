A running list, revised often. Right now: software should feel considered, not
assembled. Most bugs are communication failures wearing a stack trace. The
fastest way to learn a system is to try to delete part of it.

Constraints are a gift if you choose them, and a cage if they're chosen for you —
the work is telling the difference.

None of this is fixed. I'll disagree with half of it by next year, which is sort
of the point of writing it down.
