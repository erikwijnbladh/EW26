"Discuss video games" isn't a sloppy prompt. It's five requests wearing one
coat, and the box takes it without asking which.

Pick an ask, then tick what you meant.

<IntentField />

The readings aren't a correction — they're the writing. You're not fixing a
misunderstanding after it happens, you're choosing before one can.

I'd built a version that guessed a single intent and showed how sure it was.
It read as honest and did nothing: a number that tells you it's 44% confident
still leaves the rewriting to you. Enumerating beats scoring, because a list
you can tick is a decision and a percentage is a status report.

The classifier here is a lookup table. Swapping it for a real one is an
afternoon and wouldn't change the interaction, which is the part I wanted to
find out about.
