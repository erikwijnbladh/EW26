Describe a character and get three things back: a stat sheet, a portrait, and a
voice you can hear. Text is quick, audio is slower, images take as long as they
take.

Press forge — then tick the box and press it again.

<ParallelForge />

Each output has its own status, so nothing waits on the slowest and a failure
stays where it happened. Progress lives in the database rather than the request,
which is what lets you close the tab and come back to it.

<Image src="/images/mythforge-character.jpg" alt="A generated character page in Mythforge — portrait panel and character sheet side by side, each with its own READY badge, showing armor, hit points, ability scores and traits" />

The rest is restraint: one teal mist, heavy display type for the name, stats in
a plain numeric grid. The portrait is the only real colour on the page.
