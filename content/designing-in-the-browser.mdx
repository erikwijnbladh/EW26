I stopped designing in Figma a year ago. Not because the tool got worse, but
because the gap between what I drew and what I shipped kept growing.

Working directly in the browser means every decision is made under the real
constraints — real type rendering, real motion, real data. It's slower at first
and faster by the third iteration.

The side effect I didn't expect: the work looks less designed. Less composed. I
think that's a good sign.
