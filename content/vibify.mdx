You type something like "driving home at 2am, a bit sad but fine about it", and
it builds you a real Spotify playlist.

The prompt goes to a model, and what comes back is this:

```ts
interface PlaylistAnalysis {
  playlistName: string;
  description: string;
  seedArtists: string[];
  seedGenres: string[];
  targetEnergy: number;
  targetValence: number;
  targetDanceability: number;
  targetAcousticness: number;
  targetTempo: number;
  supplementalSearches: string[];
}
```

Which is really just a sentence translated into things Spotify's API already
knows how to answer. Those numbers go straight into the search and
recommendation endpoints, and what comes back becomes a playlist in your
account.

Almost all of the quality lives in that middle step. The same prompt with
slightly different seed artists gives you a completely different playlist, and
there's no way to tell which one was right except listening to it. I spent
most of my time there and barely any of it on the interface, which in hindsight
is the correct ratio and also why the interface looks like that.
