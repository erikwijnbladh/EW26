Nobody reads documentation. So: you paste a docs URL, and you get back five
vertical videos with Minecraft parkour footage running underneath, an AI
voiceover on top, and subtitles popping in word by word like every TikTok
you've ever scrolled past without meaning to.

Claude scrapes the page and writes the five scripts. ElevenLabs reads them out
and hands back character-level timestamps, which is the bit that makes it work
— without those the subtitles drift a syllable behind and the whole illusion
falls over. Playback is just a `<video>`, an `<audio>` and some arithmetic on
the timestamps, so there's no render pipeline and nothing to export.

It started as a joke. Then some of the scripts were genuinely a decent
explanation of the thing, which I hadn't planned for and still find slightly
annoying.
